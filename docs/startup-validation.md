# Startup validation — interview before product-building

## Customer hypothesis
Small electronics/PCB assembly shops with frequent board changeovers and limited inspection engineering capacity. Competitor benchmark: existing automated optical inspection (AOI), machine vision integrators and manual inspection.

## Discovery interview (20 minutes)
1. Walk me through your most recent inspection setup/changeover.
2. What defects matter most? Which are missed? Which cause false rejects?
3. Show me (if permitted) where reference images, QA logs and labels live.
4. How many boards/day? Current time per inspection? Cost of escapes/rework?
5. What camera/lighting and AOI tools do you use today?
6. Where are current systems too expensive, brittle or difficult to retrain?
7. Who approves software purchases and what would make them switch?
8. Would you share a small, anonymized, permitted dataset for an evaluation?

## Go / no-go pilot criteria
- At least 8 interviews with targeted operators/quality managers.
- At least 3 independently reporting the same expensive workflow gap.
- At least 1 pilot partner willing to share evaluation data under acceptable terms.
- A baseline demonstrating reduced onboarding/review effort without unacceptable false negatives.

These are **decision heuristics**, not evidence of product-market fit. Keep human QA approval mandatory. Check immigration rules and university guidance before conducting commercial business activity.
